## How to interpret citations

[S11] in the review covers S11a and S11b. Engineering lessons from web research/coding systems are proposals for mathematical workflows, not experimentally validated mathematical speedup factors. An institutional article about a mathematical result is not our independent verification of that result. The downloaded package's own code/tests are the source for implementation claims; upstream runtime behavior remains unverified.
