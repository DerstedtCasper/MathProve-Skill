# Optional 30-day research sprint — DRAFT, not scheduled or approved

Use only when the researcher chooses a month-scale project. Short routine questions should not instantiate this machinery. Calendar dates, tool/model budgets and human reviewers must be filled in by the operator.

D0–D3: lock the statement/assumptions, source coverage and novelty claim; identify one warm-up and the critical lemma. Define the smallest falsifiable milestone.

D7 review: demand a reproducible artifact, not a long transcript. Decide continue / change route / narrow scope / pause. Record the reason and the new information justifying more work.

D14 review: require a coherent dependency path and at least one serious adverse probe. Reassess which intermediate theorem has standalone value and which assumptions are doing the work.

D21 review: compare the current specification with the original problem. Freeze a candidate integration scope only when justified; schedule human review and explicit formal replay separately. Do not retroactively hide hypothesis changes.

D30 handoff: export current results, failed routes, limitations, source attributions and remaining obligations. Unresolved work may end honestly. Publishing, spending more budget or claiming novelty requires a fresh human decision.

Suggested fields per review: current goal revision; new validated obligations; unresolved counterexamples; literature gaps; attempts/provider cost; exact next task; stop trigger; reviewer; decision evidence.
