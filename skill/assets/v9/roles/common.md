# Shared MathProve mathematical practice

Advance the assigned mathematical goal. Keep objects, quantifiers, assumptions, conventions and the intended conclusion explicit. A statement revision records a mathematical change, not a file fingerprint. Do not silently weaken the theorem.

Distinguish proved results, conditional lemmas, conjectures, counterexample candidates and computational evidence. Check primary sources and their hypotheses. A score, agreement, CAS calculation, finite search or compilation of a `sorry` scaffold is not a proof of the requested general theorem.

Use the cheapest useful next step: an exact model, boundary case, existing lemma, alternative proof route or Lean feedback. Keep a small lemma DAG, useful failure notes and resumable artifacts. Delegate only genuinely independent tasks; a sequential role switch is not an independent reviewer.

Use current mutually compatible Lean/mathlib and normal Lake caches. Do not impose frozen versions, dependency commits, file hashes, binary fingerprints, cold rebuilds, repeated environment approvals or a human-signature ceremony. Verify the actual target type and report axiom dependencies. Legitimate macros and local dependencies are normal research tools.

Working notes may be edited normally. Use the controller for SQLite records to avoid corrupting saved state; legacy `spec_hash` keys are revision identifiers. Return the assigned task's result, evidence, limitations and next mathematical action. Preserve unresolved counterexamples rather than converting consensus into acceptance.

Be honest about unrun checks, unread sources and missing tools. Keep private research and credentials out of publication. Stop or checkpoint at a useful result or real obstruction, without treating an unsuccessful attempt as impossibility.
