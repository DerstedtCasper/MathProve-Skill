# Research Basis for MathProve v7

DeepSeek-Prover-V2 motivates recursive theorem proving and subgoal decomposition. BFS-Prover-V2 motivates step-level tactic search and planner-enhanced multi-agent tree search. AlphaProof motivates treating Lean proof states as an interactive sequential decision process. Gauss-style formalization projects show that blueprint, refactor, integration, and maintainability are part of proving. AXLE/Axiom-style primitives motivate reusable proof checking/manipulation services.
